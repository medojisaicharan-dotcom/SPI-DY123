EARTH-199999 // THE APARTMENT RAIN
TOM HOLLAND / NO WAY HOME

Loss of Aunt May & Forgotten Existence

WHAT HAPPENED

Peter loses Aunt May during the confrontation with Norman Osborn. Her final lesson reinforces the meaning of responsibility. Later, to prevent a multiverse collapse, Peter sacrifices his entire identity, causing MJ and Ned to forget he ever existed.

PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT

Peter briefly falls into bloodlust, nearly killing Osborn before Maguire's Spider-Man intervenes. He experiences absolute isolation in a tiny apartment, choosing not to re-introduce himself to protect his friends.

FUTURE RESOLUTION

He transforms from a high-tech protege into a pure, selfless street guardian sewing his own suit and answering police scanners alone.

EARTH-96283 // THE ALLEYWAY LESSON
TOBEY MAGUIRE / SPIDER-MAN 1 & 3

Uncle Ben & The Death of Harry Osborn

WHAT HAPPENED

Peter's choice to let a robber escape is followed by the loss of Uncle Ben. Years later, his best friend Harry Osborn gives his life to protect Peter during the final battle with Venom.

PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT

Uncle Ben's death anchors Peter's moral compass forever. Harry's sacrifice releases Peter from the poison of mutual hatred and teaches him the power of forgiveness.

FUTURE RESOLUTION

He resolves that no matter how difficult his personal life becomes, he will never abandon his duty to safeguard innocent lives.

EARTH-120703 // THE CLOCKTOWER FALL
ANDREW GARFIELD / THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN 2

The Fall of Gwen Stacy

WHAT HAPPENED

During the clocktower battle against Green Goblin, Gwen Stacy falls. Peter reaches her with a web, but cannot prevent the fatal impact.

PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT

Peter experiences immense, crushing guilt. He visits her grave daily in the freezing snow and enters a dark period where he stops holding back in combat.

FUTURE RESOLUTION

Catching MJ during the multiverse battle on Earth-199999 provides profound emotional redemption, letting him smile and believe in hope again.

Villain Counting Methodology

Villain counts across live-action Spider-Man cinema vary depending on how variants, ensemble teams, and brief cameos are cataloged. Our archive applies the following transparent criteria:

  • Confirmed Major Villains: Primary antagonists who drive major film plot arcs with dedicated combat sequences (e.g., Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Vulture, Mysterio, Electro, Lizard, Venom).
  • Multiverse Variants: Alternate-dimension villains appearing in crossover events (such as No Way Home) are counted under both their home universe and their active crossover combatant.
  • Minor / Secondary Opponents: Secondary enforcers (e.g., Shocker, Tinkerer, Rhino's brief intro) are listed as minor encounters rather than standalone major headline threats.
  • Cosmic / Ensemble Threats: Broad galactic antagonists (Thanos) fought in team settings are categorized as ensemble crossovers.

All counts are fully transparent to give visitors a clear, non-arbitrary view of each Spider-Man's rogues gallery.
